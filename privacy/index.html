Privacy policy

This policy describes how the Android game Abysse: 3D Block Puzzle (package com.allanmedeiros.abysse) handles your information.

Summary

Abysse does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for internet access.

Data kept on your device

The game saves two small files in its own private storage on your device:

  • your settings (language, vibration, controls and the last pit setup you played);
  • your high scores (score, layers, cubes, time played and date of each game).

These files never leave your device and are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission, to give a short buzz when a piece locks or a layer clears. You can turn this off in the game's Options.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Google Play

Google Play, which you use to download the game, may process data under Google's privacy policy. The game itself does not send anything to Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the developer email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
